% Astronomy's pursuit of understanding the Universe's content and growth stands as a fundamental research domain. Addressing these open questions often involves extensive hydrodynamical simulations that can track the cosmic web's evolution over time. However observationally, capturing a snapshot of the formation of large-scale structures has proven challenging and making a direct comparison between simulations and reality hard to interpret. In my research, I push observations on the most massive objects in their respective epochs to higher redshifts to better constrain their evolutionary pathways. Herefore, I specialized in using ALMA to retrieve the characteristics of early-formed galaxies and proto-clusters.
